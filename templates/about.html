ABOUT APEXCARE

Structured triage for faster attention.

ApexCare is a rule-based smart triage demonstration. It combines vital signs, nurse reassessment, doctor review, emergency registration and patient safety features into one interface.

Rule-Based Priority

Structured vital signs produce a transparent demonstration score and HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW status.

Human Review

Nurses reassess Medium patients every 30 minutes and Low patients every hour. Doctors can check patients.

Patient Safety

Emergency intake, role-based access, limited queue visibility and a nurse panic alert are included.
